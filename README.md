**Research Associate I** at the **Department of Computational and Data Sciences (CDS), Indian Institute of Science (IISc), Bangalore**.  
Doctoral thesis supervised by **[Prof. Phaneendra K. Yalavarthy](http://cds.iisc.ac.in/faculty/yalavarthy/)**.

My research combines **physics-based forward modeling**, **deep learning**, and **numerical optimization** to solve ill-posed inverse problems in **3D quantitative imaging (QSM)** and **real-time clinical ultrasound vision**.

---

### 🔬 Core Research Domains
- **Quantitative Susceptibility Mapping (QSM)**: Physics-informed unrolled architectures, Schatten $p$-norm regularization, iteration-specific denoising, and accelerated dipole inversion.
- **Ultrasound Computer Vision**: Real-time (~43 FPS) nerve segmentation and CSA measurement deployed clinically at Aster CMI Hospital.
- **Scalable 3D Inverse Solvers**: Momentum-QSM (Nesterov acceleration), Fractional-QSM (spectral conditioning), and Neumann-QSM (truncated operator series).
